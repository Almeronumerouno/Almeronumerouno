## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an Informatics undergraduate at **Universitas Multimedia Nusantara (UMN)** passionate about bridging modern web development with practical **AI/LLM orchestration**. I enjoy designing end-to-end systems—from responsive frontends to automated agentic backends.

- 🏛️ **Recent Experience:** Completed an internship at **Diskominfo Kota Tangerang**, engineering an integrated **Helpdesk TIK** platform and an AI-powered customer service chatbot.
- 🤖 **AI & Automation:** Building **RAG pipelines**, **n8n** multi-agent workflows, and experimenting with local LLM inference (**Ollama**) & CLI coding agents.
- 📈 **Current Focus:** Developing an automated **Telegram Bot for IDX/BEI swing trading signals** and market screening.
